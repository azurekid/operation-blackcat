## THE HEIST AT BUILDING 34

On the night of **March 25, 2026**, while the tech industry converged on Seattle for the annual MVP Summit, a shadow moved through the Microsoft Campus in Redmond. 

The target was not a prototype or a server, but a piece of history: **The Greenberg Memo**. 

Dated December 6, 1978, this single typed page was the blueprint for the iconic "Albuquerque 11" photograph — the image of the eleven original Microsoft employees just before they relocated to Washington. To collectors, it is the "Genesis Document" of the personal computing revolution. To the Microsoft Archive, it was the soul of Vault A.

At 23:51 UTC, the climate-controlled vault in Building 34 registered a brief pressure change. No alarms sounded. No biometric flags were raised. By morning, the vault was empty, and the security footage of the entire wing had been lost to a "storage failure."

The suspect didn't just break in; they belonged.
